🌟 The Golden Rules of Baserunning

⚡
Super Rule

WITH 2 OUTS: ALWAYS RUN IMMEDIATELY! 🏃‍♂️💨

Don't tag up! Don't go half-way! If there are 2 outs, RUN as fast as you can on ANY hit!

1. RUN! 🏃‍♂️💨

Ground Balls & 2 Outs

Ground ball on grass OR 2 OUTS? Sprint to the next base as fast as you can!

2. TAG UP! 🛑 (0 or 1 Out)

High Deep Fly Balls

Keep your foot on the base. Wait until the fielder catches the ball, then RUN!

3. HALF-WAY! ⚖️ (0 or 1 Out)

Shallow Fly Balls

Not sure if caught? Take steps halfway to the next base and WATCH the fielder!

📋 Situational Cheat Sheet by Out Count

0 OUTS or 1 OUT

Be Careful & Read the Play

Forced Runners (Runner on 1st):

Grounder? MUST RUN! Fly Ball? Tag up (deep) or Go Half-Way (shallow).

Unforced Runners (1st Base Empty):

Grounder? Hold if hit in front, run if hit behind. Runner on 3rd ALWAYS tags up on deep flies!

2 OUTS (CRUCIAL)

NO Tag Up & NO Half-Way!

Sprint on ANY hit instantly!

Why? If the fly ball is caught, it's the 3rd out and the inning ends anyway! If it's dropped, you get a huge head start to score!
